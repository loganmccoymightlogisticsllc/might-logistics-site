Dry van, reefer or flatbed: choosing the right equipment

A plain-language guide to matching your freight with the right trailer.

The right equipment protects your freight and avoids delays. Here is a simple way to think about it.

Dry van

An enclosed trailer for palletized and boxed goods that need protection from weather and theft. It is the most common choice for general freight.

Reefer

A refrigerated trailer for temperature-sensitive freight such as food, beverages and some pharmaceuticals. Confirm the required temperature range before booking.

Flatbed and step deck

Open trailers for freight that is oversized, tall, or loaded from the side or top, such as building materials and machinery. Securement and tarping needs should be agreed up front.
