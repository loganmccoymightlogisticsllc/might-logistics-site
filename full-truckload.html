Full Truckload

Your freight gets the whole trailer

When your shipment fills most of a trailer, or when you simply need it to move without being handled by anyone else, full truckload is the mode that makes sense. The truck is yours from the dock at origin to the dock at destination. Nothing is added, nothing is unloaded, and no other shipper’s freight changes your timeline.

Our job is to find the right carrier for your lane, price it fairly, and run the load so you never have to chase it. We confirm pickup and delivery windows up front, stay in touch with the driver during transit, and tell you early if something changes. You get one point of contact and straight answers.

Request a Quote

Best for

  • Shipments of roughly 10 pallets or more, or 10,000 lbs and up
  • Time-sensitive freight that cannot sit on a terminal dock
  • High-value or fragile freight that should be handled as little as possible
  • Recurring lanes where you want consistent, dependable capacity
  • Retail, manufacturing, food and consumer goods shippers

Questions

Full Truckload FAQ

How do I know if I need full truckload?

If your freight fills most of a trailer, weighs over about 10,000 lbs, or needs to ship without being handled by other parties, full truckload is usually the right choice. If you are not sure, send us the details and we will recommend the mode.

What do you need to quote a load?

Pickup and delivery locations with ZIP codes, commodity, weight, pallet count or dimensions, equipment type and pickup date. Special requirements like liftgate or appointment times help us quote it right the first time.

Can you handle recurring lanes?

Yes. Regular lanes are where we can offer the most consistency. Talk to us about your volume and we will build a plan around it.

How are drivers and carriers selected?

Carriers complete our onboarding and we verify authority and insurance before a load is offered. We match the carrier to your lane and freight.
