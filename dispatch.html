Dispatch

Dispatch that keeps your truck moving

Every trucking company we dispatch gets a dedicated dispatcher who finds, negotiates and books loads for you.

What dispatch does

We handle the phone work so you can drive

Every trucking company we work with gets one dedicated dispatcher. Your dispatcher works the load boards, negotiates the rate and keeps the paperwork moving.

🔍

Load search

We look for loads that fit your equipment, lanes and schedule, so you are not sitting on load boards all day.

🤝

Rate negotiation

We negotiate with brokers for you and hold to the minimum rate per mile you set.

📝

Paperwork

Broker setup packets, rate confirmations and load documents are handled and sent to you clean.

🛡️

Broker checks

We check a broker's authority and payment history before we book you on their freight.

📍

Check calls

We keep an eye on pickup and delivery so problems get solved early, not at the dock.

💰

Billing support

We help get proof of delivery and invoices to the broker or your factor so you get paid.

How it works

Get started in four steps

Apply online

Tell us about your truck, lanes and how you want to work.

We assign your dispatcher

Our team reviews your application and assigns you a dedicated dispatcher who calls you.

Paperwork

Send your insurance, W-9 and authority, and sign the dispatch agreement.

Your dispatcher books loads

Tell your dispatcher when your truck is open and they find and book your next load.

You stay in charge

Your authority. Your decisions.

Dispatch is its own team inside Might Logistics, separate from our freight brokerage. Your loads, rates and documents stay with dispatch.

  • You run under your own MC authority and insurance
  • We confirm each load with you before booking it
  • You set your lanes and your minimum rate per mile
  • A portal to see your loads and history (coming soon)
  • No pressure: apply, talk, then decide
Questions

Dispatch FAQ

Do I need my own MC number?

Yes. Dispatch clients haul under their own operating authority and insurance. We book and manage loads for you.

What does dispatch cost?

Fees are covered on your intro call and written into your dispatch agreement before we book your first load.

Is dispatch the same as your brokerage?

No. Dispatch is a separate team. Our dispatchers work for you and book loads from the broker community. Our brokerage team works with shippers.

Who will my dispatcher be?

Every trucking company gets one dedicated dispatcher, assigned by our team after you apply. You deal with the same person every day.

Can I bring more than one truck?

Yes. Small fleets are welcome. Tell us how many trucks you run when you apply.

What do I need to sign up?

An active MC and USDOT number. You can send your certificate of insurance, W-9 and authority letter with your application or shortly after.

How quickly can you start?

Once your paperwork is in and your dispatch agreement is signed, we can start booking loads for you.

Ready for a dispatcher?

Apply in about five minutes. A real person calls you back.
